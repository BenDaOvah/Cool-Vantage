Before you call

What areas does Cool Vantage serve?+

Cool Vantage is based in Apopka and serves homes and businesses throughout Central Florida. Call to confirm service availability for your location.

What should I do if my AC is blowing warm air?+

Check that the thermostat is set to cool, make sure the filter is not blocked, and call for service if the issue continues. Avoid repeated resets if the system is making unusual sounds or leaking.

Does Cool Vantage work on commercial refrigeration?+

Yes. The company provides light commercial refrigeration service for businesses. Call with the equipment type and symptoms so the next step can be scoped clearly.
