Before you call

What commercial refrigeration equipment do you service?+

Cool Vantage focuses on light commercial refrigeration. Call with the equipment type and location so the team can confirm whether it is within scope.

What should I do if a cooler is not holding temperature?+

Protect product according to your business procedures, note the temperature and symptoms, and call for service promptly. Avoid repeatedly cycling power if the equipment is making unusual sounds.
