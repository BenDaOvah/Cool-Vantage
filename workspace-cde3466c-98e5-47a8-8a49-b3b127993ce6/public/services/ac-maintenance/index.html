Useful answers

Before you call

How often should AC maintenance happen?+

Many homeowners schedule professional maintenance at least once a year, with timing based on the equipment, usage, and condition. Ask about a cadence that fits your system.

What can I do between visits?+

Replace the filter on the schedule your system requires, keep supply and return vents clear, and pay attention to changes in sound, airflow, or moisture.
